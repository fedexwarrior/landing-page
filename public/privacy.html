VelvetCrush Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

This policy explains what VelvetCrush (operated by [VelvetCrush]) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It applies to velvetcrush.app and works together with our Terms of Service.

1. What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and a Google account ID. We don't receive your Google password.
  • Credits and purchases. Your credit balance and a record of which purchases have been applied to your account. Payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see or store your full card number.
  • Chats. Messages you send are passed to our AI text provider to generate a reply. Our servers do not save your chat history. The conversation you see is held in your browser and disappears when you close or refresh the page. Our AI provider may keep data under its own policy.
  • Image choices. The options you pick to generate an image are sent to our AI image provider to create the picture. We also keep anonymous running totals of how often each option is picked, which are not linked to you.
  • Technical data. Like most sites, our hosting provider receives standard information such as your IP address, browser, and device. We use your IP address to limit abusive request rates.
  • Cookies. We use a sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. Google sign-in may set its own cookies.

2. How we use it

  • to run the Service, sign you in, and keep your credit balance;
  • to process payments and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • to respond to support requests;
  • to improve and fix the Service;
  • to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run VelvetCrush:

  • Google (sign-in)
  • Stripe (payments)
  • OpenRouter and the AI model providers behind it (chat replies)
  • fal.ai (image generation)
  • Vercel (hosting)
  • Upstash (database)

We may also share information if the law requires it or to protect the safety and rights of users and the Service.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we will do so within a reasonable time, except for records we must keep, such as payment records and fraud prevention. Our providers keep data under their own policies.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing [your support email]. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, and we will respond as the law requires. You can also sign out at any time and clear cookies in your browser.

6. Adults only

VelvetCrush is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we will delete it if we learn we have.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and secure sign-in cookies. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

8. Where data is processed

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
